## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to inform the user that all content present on
this website (unless mentioned otherwise) is strictly aligned with the
**Brainmade Mark** philosophy. By adhering to a guideline that prioritizes
**human effort and authenticity**, I ensure that the work consumed here carries
the integrity of human creation. The content is marked to signify it was made
**mostly by a human** (approximately 90% or more), celebrating the transcendent
value of human struggle and creativity rather than simply opposing AI.

## Scope

This policy applies to all pages and functionalities of the website containing
original or derived works, such as profiles, blog posts, quotes, and comments.
It signifies that the content was not generated by tools like GPT. Whereever I
have used AI as a tool to support my work (e.g Generating the cover image of my
blog posts), I have made it a point to mention where and how the AI content was
generated. While auxiliary tools (like using AI for a rhyming dictionary or
brainstorming synonyms) may be used to spark joy or aid the process, the core
creative output—the core idea, the content, significant portions of the
design—remains the product of human intent and labor.

## Policy

In the spirit of the Brainmade idea: **"I don't hate AIs, I love humans!"**. The
site utilises the Brainmade logo to indicate that the artwork, poetry, or prose
was made by a human, distinguishing it from content where the creator acts
merely as a consumer of AI output. However, where AI is used to enhance the
overall quality of the content delivered, I have made it a point to call it out
for absolute transparency. The source code for this site is remotely hosted as a
repository on GitHub.

Suggestions for improvement of this policy along with the necessary site wide

confidential and will not be used for any other purpose.
