#### Link Policy

Across the site, various internal and external links are used. The policies
governing these links are as follows:

**Links to Internal Sources**

- All internal links do not have any type of analytics or tracking embedded.
- Internal links provide direct access to the intended content ("what you see is
  what you get").
- JavaScript may be fetched and executed to ensure a smooth and consistent
  experience.
- All content is strictly moderated and does not include NSFW or explicit
  material.

**Links to External Sources**

- External links may contain non-free, commercial JavaScript for cookies,
  analytics, and tracking.
- No endorsement or rejection is implied for any external links, their owners,
  business models, or views unless explicitly stated.
- External content may contain text, images, or videos that could potentially
  affect the viewer's sentiments. The site assumes no responsibility for such
  impacts.
- No external links point to NSFW or explicit content. All external content is
  held to the same standards as internal content.
